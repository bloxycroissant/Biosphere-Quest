## 🚀 Overview

**Biosphere Quest** is a mobile gamified learning application for Grades 1 through 6 that gives students a window into learning. While studying foundational subjects, children learn and can see their progress in areas such as Basic Counting, Addition & Subtraction, Shapes, Fractions, and Elementary Science (Living Things, Weather, Space). The app includes course modules for structured learning alongside gamified activities, a study scheduler, daily streaks, and weekly leaderboards to encourage daily study habits and assist parents/guardians with study tracking.

## 📚 Educational Framework & Philosophy

The application is structured around constructivist learning theories and gamification mechanics tailored for young learners:
* **Curriculum Compliance:** All questions, concept hurdles, and difficulty levels mirror the official **DepEd MATATAG Curriculum** for Grades 1 through 6.
* **Scaffolding & Progression:** Adapts from basic counting, pattern recognition, and ecological awareness (Grade 1) to advanced algebraic equations, geometry, fractions, and ecological equilibrium (Grade 6).
* **Positive Reinforcement Loop:** Continuous positive reinforcement ensures students keep working even when encountering errors, with no harsh system shutdowns for low scores.

## 🕹️ Complete Mobile App & Game Mechanics

### Handled Core Mechanics
* **Lessons & Quizzes (Courses):** Students choose their grade level (Grades 1–6) and subject (Math or Science) to study bite-sized chunks of instruction (Counting and Number Sense, Shapes and Patterns, Addition and Subtraction, Fractions, Time and Measurement).
* **Mini-Game Challenges (Games):** Engaging challenges including Word Scramble, Number Ninja, and Codebreaker.
* **Study Planning:** Features weekly revision sessions and daily learning streaks.

### Player Controls and Interactions
* **Touch Navigation:** Pressing bottom navigation tabs (Home, Courses, Games, Profile).
* **Grade Filter Chips:** Select grade (Grade 1 to 6) buttons and filter subjects.
* **Text & Keyboard Input:** Inputting answers related to text input or keyboard (e.g., unscrambling lesson titles, entering profile info).
* **Button Taps:** Answer selection, quiz starts, toggling days in the schedule, and claiming badges.

## 📈 Progression, Mastery Logic & Scoring Rules

### Levels & Difficulty Scaling
* **Curriculum Levels:** Grade 1–3 (Primary / Key Stage 1) and Grade 4–6 (Intermediate / Key Stage 2).
* **Challenge Levels:** Easy (Grades 1–2), Medium (Grades 3–4), Hard/Master (Grades 5–6).
* **Difficulty Tiers:** Easy, Medium, Hard, and Impossible. Advanced tiers feature shorter time limits, fewer try limits, and harder problems.
* **Progressive Stage Progression:** Unlocking Stage 1 (Basic Concepts) automatically unlocks broader curriculum stages like Stage 2 (Intermediate Concepts). Replayability is supported anytime to improve accuracy scores, raise XP, and unlock achievement badges.

### Daily Quiz Proportional System (30 XP per Correct Answer)
* **5/5 (100%):** 150 XP
* **4/5 (80%):** 120 XP
* **3/5 (60%):** 90 XP
* **2/5 (40%):** 60 XP
* **1/5 (20%):** 30 XP
* **0/5 (0%):** 0 XP

### Win, Loss & Retry Conditions
* **Pass Conditions:** Complete daily quizzes or mini-game stages within the 3-minute time limit to gain XP, level up progress, and climb the Weekly Leaderboard podium (Top 1, 2, and 3).
* **Failure Conditions:** Wrong answers or missed time limits reduce remaining attempts or end the ongoing session. Abandoning a running puzzle or timing out awards +0 XP, requiring a retry to earn higher-tier achievement badges.

## 📋 Rules for Using the Mobile App / Game

* **Account & Access:** Users must register to access the system, log in via email/password or Google, and define their grade level and profile preferences.
* **Curriculum Progression:** Users can browse lessons by grade level (Grades 1–6) or subject (Math/Science) to maximize XP.
* **Mini-Game Rules:** Mini-games support 'all', 'easy', 'medium', 'hard', and 'impossible' settings. Abandoned quizzes or games without completion (skipped sections, blank codes, uncompleted puzzles) will not award points.
* **Scoring & Streak Retention:** XP is credited immediately upon successful answer submission. Daily streaks increase when students complete 1 or more lessons or daily challenges every 24 hours; missing a day resets the streak to 0.
* **Leaderboard Rules:** Ranks are computed weekly based on total XP collected over 7 consecutive days.

## 🛠️ Technical Architecture & Tech Stack

* **Cross-Platform Framework:** React Native with Expo SDK, ensuring fluid cross-platform compatibility (iOS & Android).
* **Language & Type Safety:** Written entirely in strict **TypeScript** to eliminate runtime type mismatches.
* **Audio Engine:** `expo-audio` integration handling low-latency sound effects.
* **Animation Framework:** React Native's native-driven `Animated` API for high-performance touch feedback and layout transitions.
